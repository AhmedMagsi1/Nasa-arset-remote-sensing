# NASA ARSET – Fundamentals of Remote Sensing

Course completed through NASA's Applied Remote Sensing Training Program (ARSET).
Certificate of completion: YEAR

## What I learned
- How satellites measure reflected energy across the electromagnetic spectrum
- The four types of resolution: spatial, spectral, temporal and radiometric
- Major missions and sensors (e.g. Landsat, MODIS, Sentinel)
- How to find and download free satellite data
- Vegetation indices such as NDVI for monitoring crops

## How I am using it
I am applying these skills in my research on early-season wheat yield
forecasting, using MODIS and Sentinel-2 vegetation indices with climate data.

## Contents
- `notes/` – my own course notes
- `exercises/` – my practice work (e.g. NDVI calculations)
- `certificate/` – certificate of completion
